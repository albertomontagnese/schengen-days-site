Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026

Schengen Days is a local-first app. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data stored on your device

Travel dates and optional trip notes are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. No passport number, identity document, account, or precise location is requested. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, location tracking, account system, or developer-operated server. Apple may provide aggregate diagnostics if you separately enable Share with App Developers in iOS settings.
