Schengen Days Support

Clear 90/180-day planning.

Email alberto.montagnese@gmail.com for help. Include your iOS version and a short description.

Add travel history

Tap + and enter each Schengen entry and exit date. Both dates count as presence days. Overlapping records are counted once.

Check a future trip

Choose proposed entry and exit dates. The app evaluates each date and shows the highest use within any rolling 180-day window touched by the trip.

Delete your data

Long-press a trip to delete it. Deleting the app removes all travel history from the device.
